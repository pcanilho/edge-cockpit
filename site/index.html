What it does

Live telemetry

Turn on the game's telemetry in Settings (by hand: setx GRPC_CLIENT_SERVER_ENABLED true on 4.10, server.game.telemetry.enabled=1 in user.cfg on 5.0, then restart the RSI Launcher and the game) and the switches show the ship's real state: power, shields, gear, lights, flight assists, SCM or NAV. The status display adds the ten warning lights, boost and speed, and on 5.0 shields, hull and radar altitude. What the game doesn't send stays blank. Telemetry off, it runs from Game.log.

Telemetry pages

Eight instrument pages: Ship, Instruments, Approach, Systems, Combat, Suit, Quantum and Turret. They show only what the game sends. The Ship page turns your ship's 3D model under your finger; guns the game names come with their rated stats.

Follows the game

Board a ship and its page opens. Step out for On foot. Go down and a revive button is waiting.

Any screen

Turn on Devices and scan the code with a phone, tablet or PC on your network. No app to install. A phone shows one plate at a time, sized for a thumb.

A page for each role

Target dial for fighters, laser power for mining, beam dial for salvage, tractor slider for cargo. Every ship page has power, flight and gear plates and Flight Ready.

Your own pages

Drag keys, switches, covers, displays and gauges onto a page. Link it to your ships, preview it on a phone, share it as a file.

Where you stand

Location, mission, injuries and hangar queue on the status display. The frame turns red for a CrimeStat or a disabled ship. Nothing is guessed.

Session log

Time played, missions, ships flown, who downed you and with what, and a timeline. Past sessions come from the game's log backups.

The world around you

Executive hangar cycle, vault and printer timers, server status, and commodity prices from UEX where you are.

Your keys

Reads your actionmaps.xml, so rebound keys just work. The game keeps focus while you tap.

  • Out of the wayStarts with Windows. Wakes with the game, dark when you quit.
  • Your layoutLong-press a key to swap it, a plate's title to move it.
  • Night modeOn, off or auto at night. Dims the backlight too.
  • UpdatesOffered between sessions, never while you play.
  • No accidentsEject is a slide. Self-destruct sits under a cover.
  • Key marksThe ? by the clock explains every mark on a key.

Install

  1. Download and run the installer SmartScreen warns, as the app isn't code signed: More info, Run anyway.
  2. Pick the control profile In game: Options › Keybindings › Advanced Controls Customization › Control Profiles. Pick edge-cockpit, tick Keyboard and Mouse. It only binds actions you left unbound.
  3. Launch Star Citizen The console wakes on the touchscreen and opens the right page.

Questions

Do I need a Xeneon Edge?

It's the screen the layout is built for. Other touchscreens work with Fit or Actual size in Settings › Screen; ask for your device. Phones, tablets and PCs join from Settings › Devices.

Can I change the pages?

Yes. Swap keys, move plates or build your own pages. Back to stock undoes it, and Share saves a page as a file.

Is it safe to use with the game?

It sends key presses like a second keyboard and reads the game's own log and telemetry. It doesn't read game memory, inject code or change game files, apart from adding its keybind profile and, if you turn telemetry on, one line in user.cfg (5.0) or one user variable (4.10).

Does it take focus from the game?

No. Mouse and keyboard stay in the game.

Does it cost anything?

No. Free, MIT licensed, no account, ads or tracking.

What does it connect to?

FleetYards.net for ship outlines, renders, 3D models and paints (kept on your PC). The Star Citizen Wiki for gun stats (every 12 h). GitHub for updates. On the World page, UEX for prices (every 30 min) and RSI's status page (every 5 min). With Devices on, your home network on port 47823; only paired devices can press keys. Nothing else.

Which game version does it support?

The current LIVE build. Each release names the build it was tested on.

Something doesn't work. Where do I report it?

Open a bug report.
