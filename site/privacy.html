Privacy

Edge Cockpit · updated 27 November 2026

Edge Cockpit collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to its author.

What stays on your PC

  • Settings, pages you build and keybind choices are stored on your PC only.
  • The app reads the game's Game.log and keybind files on your PC to follow what happens in the game. That data never leaves your PC.
  • Telemetry from the game is read over a local connection on your own PC (127.0.0.1).
  • A problem report is saved as a text file on your PC. You decide whether to share it.

What the app downloads

The app fetches public reference data. These requests send no personal data, but the services see your IP address, as with any web request:

  • Ship images and specifications from FleetYards.net.
  • Item and weapon data from the Star Citizen Wiki.
  • Commodity data from UEX.
  • Server status from the game's public status page.
  • Copies from GitHub check GitHub for new versions. Copies from the Microsoft Store are updated by the Store.

Phones and tablets

If you add a phone or tablet as an extra screen, it connects to the app over your local network only, after you pair it. Nothing goes through the internet.
